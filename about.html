About

I study Computer Science at London South Bank University, where I am in my final year. Before that, I spent a year in the Honours BSc programme in Computer Science, Mathematics and Statistics at the University of Toronto. I am a British and Canadian citizen.


My honours project tests whether scoring only the sentences that mention a keyword estimates opinion about it more accurately than scoring the whole review. After graduating, I want to work on engineering teams that design learning and inference systems.


Alongside my studies, I tutor GCSE and A-level Mathematics, Physics and Computer Science.
