About

I study Computer Science at London South Bank University, after a first year of Computer Science, Mathematics and Statistics at the University of Toronto. I’m a UK and Canadian citizen.


I’m most interested in problems that don’t have a known procedure for solving them — ones that reward careful reasoning, lateral thinking and new ideas. My current honours project looks at estimating sentiment towards specific keywords in product reviews.


Alongside my studies I tutor students from primary school to A-Level.
