Projects

Road accident severity analysis (Havering, 4,023 accidents). Using police-recorded accidents in a London borough, I mined association rules to find combinations of conditions linked to serious outcomes: 25 of 64 single-vehicle accidents in street-lit darkness near a crossing were serious, 3.25 times the overall rate. I then selected among 72 decision trees using an assumed 9:1 cost of a missed serious accident against a false alarm, which raised validation recall from 50.5% to 62.9% compared with selection by F1. Repeating the selection over 20 splits and testing on a later year showed where the model helped and where it did not.


Keyword-level sentiment analysis of Amazon reviews (honours project, in progress). I audited the Amazon Reviews 2023 software category, found that Android apps made up about 90% of its reviews, and restricted the study to about 459,000 other reviews. Because star ratings describe a whole review, I designed a separate evaluation set of review–keyword pairs for manual labelling. I have implemented TF-IDF, Naive Bayes and logistic regression from scratch in NumPy to compare with standard libraries and a pretrained transformer.


Relational database and analytics dashboard. From a business case study, I designed a 17-entity schema normalised to 3NF/BCNF, implemented it in SQL Server with constraints, a trigger and stored procedures, and built a Power BI dashboard on top of it.
